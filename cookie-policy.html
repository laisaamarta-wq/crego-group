02

Information about cookies

Cookies are small files containing letters and numbers that are downloaded and stored in your web browser when you visit the website using your computer or another device (such as a smartphone or tablet). They can be used to track which pages you visit on the website, save the information you have entered, or remember your preferences, such as language settings, when you browse the website.

03

Purpose and legal basis for the use of cookies

Cookies are necessary to make it easier for you to use our digital services and to enable us to gather information about how visitors use our website. Cookies are also used to provide you with personalised information about our products and services. We use various types of cookies, some of which are set by default (for example, ‘essential’ cookies), while we ask for your consent to use certain cookies for analytical and targeting purposes. You can give your consent through the cookie banner.
